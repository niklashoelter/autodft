No extra calculation: the spectrum is read from the S0 optimisation's frequency run and Boltzmann-weighted over conformers.
NMR
An NMR singlepoint on every optimised S0 conformer, with the singlepoint header's method or, for high accuracy, the double hybrid revDSD-PBEP86-D4/2021 / pcSseg-3 (NoFrozenCore; keeps only the singlepoint's solvation and runs on its own cores and memory, recommended 12 × 6000 MB; much more expensive; H–Kr only). Shifts are referenced to chloroform (default; its experimental shift in the calculation's solvent is added) or TMS for ¹H/¹³C, and to CFCl₃ for ¹⁹F, computed once per method in the protected reference_systems project; closed-shell molecules only.
ESD
S1 and T1 start from the lowest S0 conformer once every S0 conformer is done. Franck-Condon rates unless Herzberg-Teller is chosen; S1→Tn channels within the window use the T1 geometry and Hessian. ISC channels also get cheap FC reruns flagging width-sensitive and soft-mode-dominated rates.
Densities
Gaussian cube files from each state's energy singlepoint, kept in its job folder.
NBO
NBO 7 inside each state's energy singlepoint; natural charges are Boltzmann-weighted on the Photophysics page.
Reactivity
Adds to every relaxed and vertical singlepoint: Hirshfeld charges, %elprop dipole/quadrupole/polarizability, NBO with Wiberg bond indices, and the .gbw kept. Stability analysis on open-shell verticals; ~350–450 GB of .gbw per 1,000 molecules.

Failure circuit breaker

Job creation and submission stop automatically for an owner when too large a share of their recently finished tasks failed, so a systematic error cannot consume the whole queue. Other owners carry on. Fix the cause before resetting. A reset starts the owner's failure count afresh.

Export

Extend

Opens the submission form with this project's molecules and headers; what is already calculated is greyed out, tick more to add it. Nothing is recomputed. Only until the project is archived.

Export project

Writes into , replacing a previous export there: .csv (reported conformer per state), _all_conformers.csv, _state_analysis.xlsx (and the photophysics workbook when requested), and molecules/<id>/ with molecule.json and one folder per state and conformer (S0/conf1/opt.inp opt.out opt.xyz sp.inp sp.out …). Only the final files of successful tasks are copied; conformers with an imaginary mode get no folder and are named in the all-conformers CSV.

This is a destructive operation. After the export it will:

  1. Delete every tree of this project.
  2. Keep the database rows and flag the molecules archived: the project stays as read-only history.

Files not selected here are gone: other conformers with "Lowest conformer per state", .gbw files unless kept, failed attempts, SLURM logs, trajectories and every other by-product.
